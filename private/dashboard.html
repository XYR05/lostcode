Welcome to the Lost Code Treasure Expedition. Read the articles and swear fealty to the tournament laws.

Flag Submission Format

All salvaged flags must strictly follow this syntax:

  • • Wrapped in lostcode{...}
  • • All letters must be lowercase
  • • Words or numbers separated by underscores (_)
lostcode{this_is_a_valid_flag_1337}

Quartermaster's Hints

Every riddle contains an optional hint from the ship's Quartermaster. Unlocking hints carries an escalating treasure penalty for your crew:

  • • 1st Hint Unlocked: -5 PTS
  • • 2nd Hint Unlocked: -10 PTS
  • • 3rd Hint Unlocked: -15 PTS (escalates by -5 pts)

Once unlocked by any crewmate, the hint is revealed to your entire crew.

4-Round Tournament Structure

ROUND 1: QUALIFIERS
10 Riddles • 0 Eliminations
All crews compete for seedings.
ROUND 2: GROUP STAGE
6 Riddles • 2 Eliminations / Group
3 top crews remain alive.
ROUND 3: SEMI-FINALS
2 Riddles • 2 Eliminations
2 elite finalists advance.
ROUND 4: FINALS
1 Riddle • 1v1 Showdown
Champion claims the Lost Treasure!

Articles of Conduct

  • • Infrastructure Security: Attacking, denial-of-service, or tampering with the competition platform or server is strictly grounds for immediate scuttling (disqualification).
  • • Crew Integrity: Collusion or sharing flags across rival crews is treated as high treason. Solve riddles with your own crew.
  • • No Brute-Forcing: Automated spamming or brute-forcing flags against the submission portal is forbidden.
