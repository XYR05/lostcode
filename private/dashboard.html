EXPEDITION ROUTE & TOURNAMENT RULES

🧭 10 Crews • Physical Clue Salvage & Auto-Unlock Flow

  • Round 1 Allocation: 10 crews compete. In Round 1, each crew randomly receives 1 allocated riddle from the 10 available riddles.
  • Pre-Bound Progression: Your riddles for Rounds 2, 3, and 4 are pre-bound in advance to your crew's journey.
  • Physical Field Salvage: Clues lead to physical campus/field locations. Find the physical answer in the field.
  • Flag Submission & Auto-Unlock: Paste your physical field answer into the flag placeholder (e.g. lostcode{field_answer}). Submitting the correct flag automatically unlocks the next round on the map and delivers your next pre-allocated riddle immediately!
ROUND 1: SETTING SAIL: 10 Crews • 10 Riddles • 0 Eliminations
Each crew solves their randomly assigned physical field riddle. All 10 crews advance upon solving.
ROUND 2: HIGH SEAS: 2 Groups of 5 Crews (Group A & Group B) • 6 Riddles
First 3 crews from each group to solve advance (6 crews advance). The remaining 2 crews per group are automatically eliminated (Walk the Plank!).
ROUND 3: DEAD MAN'S CREEK: 2 Groups of 3 Crews (Group A & Group B) • 2 Riddles
First 1 crew from each group to solve advances (2 finalists advance). The other 2 crews per group are automatically eliminated.
ROUND 4: TREASURE ISLAND ❌: 1 v 1 Final Showdown • 1 Riddle
Head-to-head championship showdown between Group A and Group B finalists. The first crew to submit claims the Grand Lost Treasure!

Flag Submission Format

All salvaged flags must strictly follow this syntax:

  • • Wrapped in lostcode{...}
  • • All letters must be lowercase
  • • Words or numbers separated by underscores (_)
lostcode{this_is_a_valid_flag_1337}

Quartermaster's Hints

Every riddle contains an optional hint from the ship's Quartermaster. Unlocking hints carries an escalating treasure penalty for your crew:

  • • 1st Hint Unlocked: -5 PTS
  • • 2nd Hint Unlocked: -10 PTS
  • • 3rd Hint Unlocked: -15 PTS (escalates by -5 pts)

Once unlocked by any crewmate, the hint is revealed to your entire crew.

🧭 Expedition Rules & Physical Field Protocol

10 Enlisted Pirate Crews: Ten crews compete across treacherous waters for the ultimate treasure bounty.

  • Round 1 Random Riddle: In Round 1, each crew randomly receives 1 allocated riddle from the 10 available riddles.
  • Pre-Bound Progression: Your riddles for subsequent rounds (Rounds 2, 3, and 4) are locked and pre-bound in advance to your crew's charted journey.
  • Physical Field Search: Clues direct your crew to physical locations across campus/field. Search the physical terrain to discover the answer.
  • Flag Placeholder Submission: The answer found in the field is pasted directly into the flag placeholder (format: lostcode{field_answer}).
  • Automatic Round Unlock: Submitting the correct physical field answer automatically unlocks the next round on your chart and instantly reveals your crew's next pre-bound riddle!
